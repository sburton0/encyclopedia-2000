# Encyclopedia 2000 Privacy Policy

**Effective October 18, 2026**

Encyclopedia 2000 is a free desktop encyclopedia for Windows, Mac, and Linux. It's built to respect your privacy: it has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking, and it doesn't collect or sell any personal information.

## What stays on your computer

The app saves a few things so it works the way you left it. They're stored only on your computer and are never sent anywhere:

- Your settings from Tools → Options, such as sound, text size, and startup choices
- Your favorites and your recently viewed articles
- Game progress, such as Star Chart stars and score
- What you enter in activities, such as the birthday in The Day You Were Born and the people in Lifelines
- The window's size and position

The installed version keeps these in your user profile on your computer. The portable version keeps them in an "Encyclopedia 2000 Data" folder next to the program.

To remove them, use **Tools → Options → Privacy → Clear Recently Viewed** or **Reset All Settings**, uninstall the app, or (for the portable version) delete the "Encyclopedia 2000 Data" folder. Uninstalling may leave your settings folder behind; you can delete it from `%APPDATA%\encyclopedia-2000`.

## What the app connects to

To show you articles and keep itself up to date, the app connects to these services. Like any website you visit, they can see your computer's IP address and the request being made (for example, which article you opened). The app identifies itself to them as Encyclopedia 2000 and its version number. It doesn't send your name, email, or any other personal information.

| Service | Why |
| --- | --- |
| **Wikipedia** (en.wikipedia.org) | Articles, search, featured content, and "On this day" events |
| **Wikimedia Commons** (upload.wikimedia.org and commons.wikimedia.org) | Pictures, videos, and sounds, and the Media of the Day and Featured Sounds lists |
| **Wikidata** (query.wikidata.org) | Facts in World Explorer and Lifelines |
| **GitHub** (github.com) | Checking for and downloading updates |

These services are covered by their own privacy policies: the [Wikimedia Foundation Privacy Policy](https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Privacy_policy) for Wikipedia, Wikimedia Commons, and Wikidata, and the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) for GitHub.

You can turn off automatic update checks in **Tools → Options → Privacy**. The portable version only checks for updates; it never downloads them.

## Links that open in your web browser

Some buttons open web pages in your own browser instead of inside the app, such as **View on Wikipedia**, **Donate**, and **Send Feedback**. Once you're on those sites, their own privacy policies apply. In particular:

- **Send Feedback** offers our **Discord server** and a **Google Form**. The form is anonymous unless you choose to enter an email address for a reply. It fills in your app version and operating system version for you; nothing is sent until you press Submit. Google's and Discord's privacy policies apply to what you share there.
- **Donate** offers Wikipedia's donation page (run by the Wikimedia Foundation) and a tip page for the developer on **Ko-fi**. The app doesn't know whether you donate, and nothing about you is sent. The Wikimedia Foundation's and Ko-fi's privacy policies apply to any donation.

## This website

encyclopedia2000.com uses no cookies and no advertising or cross-site trackers. It uses **Cloudflare Web Analytics** to count visits: which pages are viewed, which site you came from, and your general country and type of device. It doesn't use cookies, doesn't follow you across other sites, and doesn't collect personal information. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and served through Cloudflare. These analytics apply only to the website; the Encyclopedia 2000 app itself has none.

## Beta sign-up emails

If you ask for the download link on our beta sign-up page, we use **Kit** (kit.com), an email service, to send it. Kit stores your email address and the date you signed up, and whether you asked for beta news. We use your address only to send the download link and, if you ticked the box, beta news. We never sell or share it, and the app itself never sees it.

If you didn't ask for beta news, we delete your address within 90 days. If you did, we keep it until you unsubscribe with the link at the bottom of every email, and delete it within 90 days after that. Kit's [privacy policy](https://kit.com/privacy) also applies. Sign-up is for people 13 and older.

## Children

The Encyclopedia 2000 app doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children. The beta sign-up page is for people 13 and older.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date, and noted in the release notes.
